Ecosystem

The National Science Foundation (NSF) Harnessing the Data Revolution (HDR) Institutes are an integrated fabric of interrelated institutes that aim to accelerate discovery and innovation in multiple areas of data-intensive science and engineering.

Learn about HDR

Meet the Institutes

Accelerated AI Algorithms for Data-Driven Discovery

A3D3 aims to construct the knowledge essential for real-time applications of artificial intelligence in three fields of science: high energy physics, multi-messenger astrophysics and systems neuroscience.

Institute for Data Driven Dynamical Design

ID4 focuses on using the power of big data to create new approaches and tools for designing materials and structures from atoms to skyscrapers.

Institute for Geospatial Understanding through an Integrative Discovery Environment

I-GUIDE drives discovery and innovation by harnessing the geospatial data revolution to advance convergence science.

Institute for Harnessing Data and Model Revolution in the Polar Regions

iHARP harnesses the power of data to create the next generation of climate modeling. Through multidisciplinary teams, their focus is on data, data and modeling, prediction, and scalability with an overarching commitment to education and outreach.

Imageomics Institute

The Imageomics Institute establishes a new scientific field called "imageomics," harnessing image data and machine learning algorithms to extract and compute biological traits for a deeper understanding of biology and the rules of life on Earth.
